# Quick 261021-gy4: Fix Statistics page accuracy

Audit 2026-10-21 (read-only DB check) found the Statistics tab over-counting and mislabelled.

## Tasks

1. **Count each promo once (dailyHistory.ts).** Within the selected window keep one observation per promo — its best profit, placed on the day it was seen (ties: earliest day). Totals, by-sport/type/book, averages, promo count and bonus/boost conversion all use the de-duplicated rows. Live check: all-time available $213.74 (60 rows) -> $139.93 (36 promos).
2. **Runs from scrape_runs (get-stats.ts, profitStats.ts, new loader).** "Scrape runs", "First run", "Latest run" come from distinct `scrape_runs.ran_at` in the range. Time-of-day averages: each scrape run since the first snapshot ever is a run; its total is the sum of snapshots captured within 30 minutes after it (0 when none), bucketed by the Denver hour of the run.
3. **Any-game promos in snapshots (promoObservations.ts).** Promos with scope "any" are recorded with sport key `any` (column is NOT NULL; no migration) and shown as "Any sport". Going forward only.
4. **"Available now" checks expiry (promoFunnel.ts, profitHistory.ts).** Active only when status is active and `expires_at` is null or in the future; same for per-book active.
5. **Labels.** Page note: group-wide (every member), each promo at its own max stake against every hedge book. Sport/weekday table: promos covering several days sit on the window's first day. History: "Each promo counted once, on the day it showed its best profit"; "N promos" instead of "N records".

npm test, tsc, lint green; re-run the read-only DB numbers against the new functions.
