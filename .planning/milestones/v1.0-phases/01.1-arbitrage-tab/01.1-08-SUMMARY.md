## Decisions Made

- **No arbs in the live feed at verification time is correct behavior, not a defect.** After the owner's approval message noted "no arb opportunities are showing," an orchestrator-run diagnostic queried the live cache directly: 92 moneyline events, 92 extended (spreads/totals) events, across the 7 free-tier books, yielding 357 two-way markets (87 moneyline, 131 spread, 139 total), of which 306 had a cross-book pair to compare. Zero markets had an implied probability sum below 1.0 (the D-04 arb threshold). The closest market was Iowa @ Michigan moneyline, hardrockbet +200 vs. espnbet -200, which is an exact break-even (implied sum = 1.0000) -- correctly excluded by the strict `< 1.0` threshold rather than a `<= 1.0` threshold. The next-closest markets clustered around 1.005-1.012 implied sum. Conclusion: `findArbs`/`calculateArb`/`findBestArbPair` are behaving exactly as designed against real market odds; there were simply no true arbitrage opportunities present in the CO market at the moment of the live fetch. No code change was made or needed.
