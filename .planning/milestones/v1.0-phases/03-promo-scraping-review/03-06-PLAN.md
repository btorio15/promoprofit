## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-06-01 | Denial of Service | fetchRequest / run | mitigate | 30s timeout, 5MB body cap, 15-min job timeout, per-book try/catch isolation, detail requests capped per book (T-3-dos) |
