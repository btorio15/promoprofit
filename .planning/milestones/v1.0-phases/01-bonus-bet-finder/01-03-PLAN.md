## Phase Goal

**As a** group member holding a bonus bet at a Colorado sportsbook, **I want to** enter the book and bonus amount and see the best market to convert it on and the best book to hedge at, **so that** I can lock in a guaranteed profit with exact stakes computed from real cached Colorado odds.
