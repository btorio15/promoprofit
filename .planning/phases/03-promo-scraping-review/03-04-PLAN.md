Vertical slice 2, "active promos show their exact hedge". This plan reads active promos from the database, resolves each one's game/market/side in the cached odds, finds the best hedge among the member's books, and computes a boost hedge (Plan 02 engine) or a bonus-bet hedge. The results render as compact expandable rows on the Promos tab.

Purpose: CALC-02 and CALC-03 reach the user, as D-01 describes. Once the scraper (Plan 06) or a reviewer (Plans 07/09) makes a promo active, a hedge appears here with no further wiring.

</objective>

- Find the event by id.
- moneyline: read each bookmaker's "h2h" market. Skip any bookmaker whose market doesn't have exactly 2 outcomes. "home" means the outcome named event.home_team.

rankPromoHedges.ts is pure:
