Aliases per team: nickname ("Broncos"), city or region ("Denver") only where unique within the league, standard abbreviations ("DEN"), and common short forms ("Niners", "Sixers", "Cards" only where unique). Do NOT add a city alias shared by two teams in the same league (LA, NY, Chicago in MLB, etc.).

resolveTeam, in order, collecting canonical names:
1. Exact normalized match against knownTeams in scope (sportKey or all).
2. The TEAM_ALIASES lookup.
3. For any sport, including NCAA, a unique-affix rule: a known team whose normalized name starts with text + " " or ends with " " + text. Only when exactly one known team in scope matches.
Return the distinct union. knownTeams is built by the matcher from the events passed in, so NCAA needs no static table. No fuzzy/edit-distance matching (RESEARCH.md Don't Hand-Roll, Anti-Pattern 2).

matcher.ts is pure; `now` is injected.
- The pool is events from moneyline ∪ extended, deduped by id, with commence in (now, now + windowDays (default 7) days], sport in SPORT_KEYS, filtered by sportKeyHint when non-null.
- Resolve selectionText (moneyline/spread) and opponentText, or teamsText (total).
- teamMatch = every required text resolved to exactly one name, and at least one pool event contains the resolved team(s), with the opponent as the other side when given.
- teamCandidates = those events.
- dateMatch: if startsAt is present, keep candidates on the same America/Denver calendar day (use Intl.DateTimeFormat with timeZone "America/Denver") and require exactly 1. Otherwise require teamCandidates.length === 1.
- side: home/away from the resolved selection team. For totals, over/under from selectionText ("over"/"o", "under"/"u").
- marketMatch = the side is determinable and resolveSelection(eventsFor(marketType), {eventId, marketType, line, side}) is non-null. eventsFor is moneyline events with extended fallback for moneyline, and extended for spread/total.
- matched only when all three are true.
- guess = the earliest remaining candidate with a determinable side, else null.
- Doc comment: this all-three rule IS the D-10 auto-accept threshold. It is deterministic, so there is no numeric score to slide, and tuning means growing aliases and hardening date logic (RESEARCH.md).
