<objective>
Make profit recording failure-safe end to end (audit W1 and W2), and fix the two stale job comments.

Purpose: The Dashboard's grey line and available totals (STATS-02, STATS-08) depend on member observations being recorded. Today a read error in the Statistics recorders can skip the whole member pass in the scheduled job, and a failure in the Dashboard's side recording fails the whole Dashboard load. This plan makes every recorder never throw, isolates the job steps, and has the Dashboard reuse odds it already loaded.
Output: hardened promoObservations.ts and morningObserve.ts, a new shared member recorder (src/db/memberFeedRecording.ts) that Plan 02 reuses for the Promos tab, updated getOpportunities, tests for each.
</objective>
