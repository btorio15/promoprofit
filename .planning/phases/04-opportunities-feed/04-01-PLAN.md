<objective>
Phase goal (user story):

**As a** group member, **I want to** open the app and immediately see the best guaranteed-profit opportunities at my own sportsbooks, ranked by profit or ROI, **so that** I can pick a bet and place it without hunting through tabs.

This plan delivers the first vertical slice: a new Opportunities tab (first tab, default on open) with the moved profit summary, a Profit/ROI sort switch remembered per device, and a "Best promos" section (top 5 own-book promo hedges) with a See all link. It builds the sources abstraction (D-02) so later plans add Best arbs and Best pairs as new union members without reworking the screen.

Purpose: DASH-01 (ranked feed, sortable), DASH-03 (own books on both sides).
Output: getOpportunities server action, pure ranking helpers, Opportunities UI wired into AppShell.
</objective>
