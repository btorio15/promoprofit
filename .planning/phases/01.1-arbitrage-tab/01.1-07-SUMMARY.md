## Accomplishments

- `ArbScreen.tsx` renders the "Arbitrage" header (h1-equivalent, verbatim UI-SPEC description) and mounts `ArbForm`.
- `ArbForm.tsx` ("use client") holds `totalStake`/`precision` in `usePersistentString` (default `"200.00"`/`"whole"`, coerced to `"whole"|"cents"`), auto-calls `findArbs` on mount and on every valid change (debounced ~300ms via a cleared `setTimeout`), guards out-of-order responses with a `requestIdRef` counter, and re-runs (with the first run swallowed, mirroring `FinderForm`) whenever `recomputeKey` changes. The controls `Card` holds the Total stake `Input`, the Whole dollars/Exact cents `ToggleGroup`, its helper caption, and the outline "Search spreads & totals" `Button` plus its always-shown ~3x credit-estimate line. The button is disabled (UX only) when `status.level === "blocked"`, when `status.remaining` is below the extended estimate, or while a search is pending. The account-risk advisory (`Alert`, no variant, verbatim copy) renders directly under the card in every state, including both empty states, satisfying SC3 without scrolling.
- `SearchSpreadsTotalsDialog.tsx` is a structural copy of `RefreshConfirmDialog`: a real in-page `AlertDialog` (never `window.confirm`) with the exact heading/body copy (including the "Your credit balance appears after the first refresh." branch when `remaining` is null, and the "last fetched N min ago" append only when known), "Cancel" (ghost) and "Search anyway" (shows "Searching…" while pending) buttons, and an `onOpenChange` guard against closing mid-request.
- `findArbs`'s `"invalid"` status routes to the same field-error paragraph as client-side validation failures; `refreshSpreadsTotals`'s `confirm_required`/`ok`/`blocked`/`busy`/`error` outcomes are each handled distinctly -- `ok` calls `router.refresh()` and `onSearched()` (bumping `AppShell`'s `recomputeKey` so both tabs recompute), `blocked` shows the exact "Only {remaining} credits left…" copy, `busy`/`error` show a destructive `Alert` with a "Try again" button.
- `describeExtendedOddsAge` (new, TDD RED->GREEN) is a pure sibling of `describeOddsAge`, reusing `STALE_AFTER_MINUTES` and identical floor-minutes math, with its own not-fetched/fresh/stale label copy.
- `OddsStatusBar` gained `showExtendedAge` (default `false`): when true it renders a second line below the moneyline age line using `describeExtendedOddsAge` on `status.extendedOddsFetchedAt` and the existing 60s tick -- amber `TriangleAlert` + "may be phantom arbs, refresh before betting" when stale, muted text otherwise (including the never-fetched case). The moneyline line, Refresh odds button, credit meter, and `handleOutcome` are all byte-unchanged.
- `FinderScreen.tsx` is now the Bonus-bets panel only (header + `FinderForm`); `OddsStatusBar`/`Separator`/`CreditBanner` moved to the new `AppShell.tsx`, which owns `recomputeKey` and `activeTab` ("bonus" default, D-09, not URL-synced) and renders both `TabsContent` panels with `keepMounted` so neither tab's state is discarded when the user switches.
- `page.tsx` renders `<AppShell status={status} bonusBooks={bonusBooks} hasCachedOdds={freshness !== null} />` in place of the old direct `FinderScreen` render; `force-dynamic` and the `Promise.all` loaders are unchanged.
