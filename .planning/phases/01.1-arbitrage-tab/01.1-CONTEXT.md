# Phase 01.1: Arbitrage Tab - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-25
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Add a top-level **Arbitrage** tab next to the bonus-bet finder. It lists cross-book sure bets (no promos) among the API-covered Colorado books and computes from cached odds. The tab shows:
- exact stakes for both legs from a user-entered total stake
- guaranteed profit and return %
- the odds age, prominently
- an account-limiting advisory

Moneyline arbs use the odds the existing refresh already fetches, so they cost no extra credits. Spreads and totals are fetched only when the user presses a separate, explicitly confirmed "Search spreads & totals" button.

This phase also folds in the pending **max hedge amount** todo for the bonus-bet finder (see Folded Todos).

**Carried forward from Phase 1 (unchanged, not re-decided):**
- **Sports:** NFL, NBA, MLB, NCAAF and NCAAB, from the configurable list.
- **Game window:** games in the next 7 days.
- **Out of season:** sports are skipped using the free `/sports` call.
- **Books:** the 7 free-tier Colorado books.
- **Credits:** warn under 100, block under 20.
- **Repeat refresh:** an in-page confirm within 15 minutes of the last one, never `window.confirm`.
- **Odds age:** turns amber after 2 hours.
- **Money math:** decimal.js with cent-exact results.
- **Refreshes:** serialized by the `refresh_lock` table.
- **Credit records:** the monthly-reset and missing-header rules from the Phase 1 code-review fixes.
- **Book lists:** always read from `src/config/books.ts`.
- **Finder:** NFL "Tie risk" treatment unchanged.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Stake input & rounding
- **D-01:** Arbs are sized from a **single total stake** that the user enters. The app splits it across both legs.
- **D-02:** Stake precision is a **toggle**. The default is **whole dollars**: each leg is rounded to whole dollars, and the display shows the exact resulting **worst-case** profit and return. A switch changes to **exact cents**, which gives equal profit either way, like the finder. The math stays in decimal.js either way. When rounding makes the two outcomes differ, the headline shows the lower one, which is the Phase 1 rule.
- **D-03:** The total stake (arb tab), the max-hedge amount (finder) and the precision toggle are **remembered in this browser** (localStorage). They are not saved in the DB. Per-user persistence can come with Phase 2 login.

### What counts as an arb
- **D-04:** Every **true arb above 0%** return is shown, with no minimum-return filter.
- **D-05:** **No near-arbs.** Only positive-return arbs at cached odds are listed.
- **D-06:** The two legs must be at **different books**. There are no same-book arbs, unlike the finder's D-17.
- **D-07:** Show **one row per game and market**, using the best price on each side, which is the finder's D-08 pattern. If other books offer **exactly the same odds** as the best price on a side, the row shows a **"Multiple books"** affordance that expands to list them. Only exact ties are listed, not near prices.
- **D-08:** Market rules must be two-way with no push:
  - **Moneylines:** the Phase 1 h2h rules.
  - **Spreads and totals:** **half-point lines only**, and the **same line must be matched exactly across books**. For example, Team A +3.5 at one book must be paired with Team B −3.5 at another, and Over 44.5 with Under 44.5.

### Tab layout & rows
- **D-09:** **Top-level tabs "Bonus bets | Arbitrage"** sit under the shared status bar. The odds age, credit meter and Refresh odds button are shared. Use the existing shadcn Tabs component. URL sync is not required.
- **D-10:** Rows **mirror the finder's compact rows**:
  - **Collapsed:** the game and time, side A book and odds, side B book and odds, profit $, return %, and a market badge.
  - **Expanded:** both stakes and the per-outcome payouts and net.
- **D-11:** Rows are sorted by **return %**, highest first.
- **D-12:** The tab has the same **sport sub-tabs with counts** as the finder: All · NFL · NCAAF · MLB · NBA · NCAAB. Switching is client-side and instant, with no re-search.

### Spreads/totals fetch
- **D-13:** A separate **"Search spreads & totals"** button fetches **all in-season sports** in one press. It has its own credit estimate and uses the same credit gate (warn/block), 15-minute confirm and `refresh_lock` as the normal refresh.
- **D-14:** That button fetches **all three markets (h2h, spreads, totals) together**, so every market in the fetch shares one timestamp. Credit cost is about 3× the normal refresh. The confirm dialog must show the estimate.
- **D-15:** Spread and total arbs appear in the **same ranked list** as moneyline arbs, each row tagged with a **market badge**: "Moneyline", "Spread ±X.5" or "Total O/U X.5".
- **D-16:** The normal **Refresh odds** still fetches h2h only. Cached spreads and totals are **kept**, with their **own "spreads/totals updated N min ago" age**, which turns amber after 2 hours like moneylines. A normal refresh does not clear them.

### Claude's Discretion
- **Rounding tiebreak:** how whole-dollar rounding picks which leg rounds up or down. Maximize the worst-case profit, and show the exact result.
- **Stake default:** the default total stake and the validation limits. Reuse the finder's amount validation style.
- **Warnings:** where the account-limiting advisory and the odds-age warning sit on the tab. Both must be clearly visible. The roadmap success criterion requires odds age to be **prominent**, because stale cached odds produce phantom arbs.
- **Empty states:** the empty-state copy, for example "No arbs right now. Try refreshing, or search spreads & totals".
- **Caching:** how spread and total odds are stored. They can go in the existing `cached_odds` table or alongside it, and should keep the Phase 1 latest-batch and purge semantics per market group. The researcher and planner decide, and must not break the finder's WR-01 latest-batch filter.
- **Credit estimate:** reuse and extend `estimateRefreshCredits` for the per-market cost.

### Folded Todos
- **Add max hedge amount input to bonus-bet finder** (`.planning/todos/pending/2026-09-25-add-max-hedge-amount-input-to-bonus-bet-finder.md`)
  - **The problem:** the finder assumes the user can fund any hedge stake. A large bonus can need an $800+ hedge.
  - **Folded in as:**
    - **D-17:** The finder gets an optional **max hedge amount**, **hidden behind a checkbox** (for example "Limit hedge amount") so the form stays uncluttered.
    - **D-18:** When it is set, **rows whose hedge stake is over the cap are hidden**. There are no partial or scaled hedges, so everything shown is still a guaranteed profit. The finder still returns up to 10 results overall and per sport **after** the filter, so the cap is applied before the top 10 is taken. The empty state explains when nothing fits under the cap.
    - **D-19:** The value is remembered in this browser (D-03).
  - The Arbitrage tab needs no separate max-stake box, because its total stake already is the cap (D-01).

</decisions>

## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- `src/domain/hedge/americanOdds.ts` (`americanToDecimal`, precision-40 clone): use it for implied-probability and arb detection. Don't re-implement it.
- `src/domain/hedge/marketFilter.ts` (`extractTwoWayMoneylines`): this is the pattern for the two-way no-push filter. Spreads and totals need a sibling extractor that matches points exactly.
- `src/domain/hedge/bonusBet.ts`: this is the pattern for cent rounding with a decimal clean-snap before the final rounding (the Phase 1 precision bug fix). Arb stake math must follow the same discipline.
- `src/app/actions/find-hedges.ts`: `resultsBySport` holds the top 10 overall and per sport, independently ranked. Mirror this for arbs, and apply the finder's max-hedge filter before ranking.
- `src/components/finder/ResultsList.tsx`, `ResultRow.tsx`, `ResultDetails.tsx` and `EmptyState.tsx`: these are the row, expand and sport-tab UI to mirror or share.
- `src/components/ui/tabs.tsx`: already used for the sport tabs. Use it for the top-level Bonus bets | Arbitrage tabs too.
- `src/ingestion/odds/quota.ts` (`evaluateRefreshGate`, `estimateRefreshCredits`), `refresh.ts` (`runOddsRefresh`, `refresh_lock`), `client.ts` (currently hardcodes `markets=h2h`) and `store.ts`: extend these for the spreads/totals fetch rather than forking them.
- `src/components/finder/RefreshConfirmDialog.tsx` and `OddsStatusBar.tsx`: reuse them for the spreads/totals confirm and the second odds-age line.

### Established Patterns
- **Server actions:** they validate input with Zod and return a discriminated union status (`ok` / `invalid` / …).
- **Page rendering:** the page is `force-dynamic`, and the build must pass without `DATABASE_URL`.
- **Tests:** Vitest with mocked `@/db/queries` and a mocked Odds API client. Tests never make live API calls.
- **Book lists:** always come from `src/config/books.ts` (the WR-05 fix).

### Integration Points
- `src/app/page.tsx` → `FinderScreen`: add the top-level tabs here, or in a new screen wrapper, with the Arbitrage panel beside the finder.
- New server action, for example `findArbs`: it reads cached odds, including spreads and totals when present, and returns arbs by sport.
- New server action, for example `refreshSpreadsTotals`, or a markets parameter on `refreshOdds`. Either way it goes through the same gate and lock.

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The owner didn't want the max-hedge box cluttering the finder form, which is why it sits behind a checkbox.
- On the Arbitrage tab, "Multiple books" is shown only when other books tie the best price exactly. It exists so the owner can place the bet wherever they actually have an account, until Phase 2 adds "my books".
- Whole-dollar stakes are the default because precise-cent bets look like arbing to the books.

</specifics>
