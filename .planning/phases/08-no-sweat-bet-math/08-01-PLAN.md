    6. Score = Decimal.min(winNet(H), loseNet(H, V)); keep the max score; tie -> smaller hedgeStake (bonusBet.ts rule).
    7. Return all fields recomputed at the chosen H: profitIfWin = winNet(H), profitIfLose = loseNet(H, V), estimatedProfit = min of those two, worstCaseProfit = Decimal.min(winNet(H), loseNet(H, 0)) (D-02, not separately optimized).
