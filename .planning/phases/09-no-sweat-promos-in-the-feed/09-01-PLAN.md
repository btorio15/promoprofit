<objective>
Introduce `no_sweat` as a real promo type and make it structurally impossible for a no-sweat to leak into guaranteed numbers: narrow the guaranteed ranking type, exclude no-sweats from `getActivePromos` in SQL, add a separate `getActiveNoSweatPromos`, and exclude no-sweats from the Statistics funnel/daily-history queries.

Purpose: FEED-04 (estimates never touch Dashboard totals, grey line, Statistics) and FEED-02 (a no-sweat is never priced as a bonus bet). Implements D-12, D-19, A-01, part of A-02.
Output: widened types, leak-proof DB reads, tests proving the exclusion.
</objective>
