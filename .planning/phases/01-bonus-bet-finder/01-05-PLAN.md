## Phase Goal

**As a** group member holding a bonus bet at a Colorado sportsbook, **I want to** enter the book and bonus amount and see the best market to convert it on and the best book to hedge at, **so that** I can lock in a guaranteed profit with exact stakes computed from real cached Colorado odds.

<objective>
Close the loop in the UI. The user can see how old the odds are and how many credits remain (Task 1), and can press Refresh odds with the confirm, block, and error flows so results recompute (Task 2). The phase then ends with a human walkthrough (Task 3).

Purpose: ODDS-02/03/04 user-facing. It completes all five ROADMAP Phase 1 success criteria.
Output: status bar, banners, confirm dialog, and a finder screen composition that recomputes after refresh.
</objective>
